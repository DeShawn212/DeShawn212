Have you tried playing the New Super Mario Bros Wii?

If you haven't tried it yet, you're in for a spectacular treat! Released in 2009, this exciting, interactive game offers a fun, creative way to connect with friends and family. I highly recommend giving it a try‐you’re sure to have a great time. Get ready for an entertaining experience packed with fun, right at your fingertips. It's family-friendly and suitable for all ages.


Discover the exciting world of star coins! How many do you think there are?

In New Super Mario Bros. Wii, you'll collect an impressive 231 Star Coins as you make your way through Worlds 1‐9. Just imagine all the exciting possibilities that come with gathering them all!!


Are star coins essential for achieving success in the game?

Yes, collecting Star Coins as you make your way through each level is essential. They’ll help you unlock secret pathways and hidden worlds. There are nine worlds, each with three Star Coins. Try to collect them all along the way. Your goal is to defeat Bowser and his friends, gather every Star Coin, and rescue the Princess from the castle. And, of course, aim to complete the game 100%.
