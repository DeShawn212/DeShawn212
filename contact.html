What are some problems with the game?


The difficulty levels from World 9

Yes, our team decided to add some hard levels ‐ to make the game fun, challenging, and a test (well not nesscary a test) but of your in-game knowledge. As you continue playing and progress, you’ll encounter hard levels in World 9. We decided to remove checkpoint flags from these levels to test your knowledge and skills. If you lose a live, you have to restart from the very beginning. And that can be frustrating, of course!
